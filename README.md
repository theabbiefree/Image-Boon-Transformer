# 🎨 Image Boon Transformer

> *Free, offline, open source bulk image tool for artists.*

**Image Boon Transformer** is a single HTML file that handles everything artists need to prep images for social media — resize, watermark, and export in bulk. No limits. No signup. No internet required. Just open and create.

Built by **abbies®** — for every artist who has ever done this one image at a time.

---

## ✨ What it does

Drop in 100+ images in any mix of PNG, JPG, or WEBP and walk away with a ZIP file of perfectly prepared social media images — all watermarked, all resized, all ready to post.

- 📐 **Bulk resize** to exact pixels — 1080×1350, square, stories, or custom
- 🖊️ **Watermark / signature** — upload your own or use the default
- 🎯 **6 placement positions** — top left, top center, top right, bottom left, bottom center, bottom right
- 🎚️ **Opacity and size controls** for your watermark
- 🔄 **Auto removes white backgrounds** from watermark files
- 💾 **Download everything as a ZIP** — one click, all done
- 🔒 **100% offline** — your images never leave your computer

---

## 🎯 Step by step

**Step 1 — Drop your images**
Drag and drop your images onto the upload area — PNG, JPG, WEBP, any mix, any size, 100+ at once.

**Step 2 — Set your watermark**
Your own signature or logo is already preloaded. Or upload your own. Pick your placement position from the 6 point grid. Adjust opacity and size to taste.

**Step 3 — Choose output settings**
Pick your output size and format. Default is 1080×1350 Instagram portrait. JPG, PNG, or WEBP output supported.

**Step 4 — Transform**
Hit the big orange button. Watch the progress bar. Download your ZIP.
